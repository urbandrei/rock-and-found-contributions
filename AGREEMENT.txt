ROCK AND FOUND: CONTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

Version 1, October 7, 2026.

This agreement is between you (the contributor) and Andrei Roman, who makes Rock and Found
(the game). You agree to it by replying "I agree" to the message or form that links here,
with the name you want credited, or by choosing "I agree" in the Rock Lab.

It covers your contributions from before you agreed as well as everything after.

If a later version of this agreement is published, it applies to what you contribute after
you accept it. This version keeps covering what you contributed before.


1. WHAT THIS COVERS

"Your contributions" means anything you send for the game or make with its tools, including:

  a. translations of the game's text into any language;
  b. suggested edits to the game's writing: corrections, rewordings, notes on tone or
     accuracy, and fact checks;
  c. new rocks, minerals, fossils and other specimens: their shapes, colors, textures,
     names, descriptions, test results and other data;
  d. maps and layouts: room plans, shelf arrangements, sorting orders and similar work;
  e. anything you make, rank, arrange or write in the Rock Lab, including votes, orderings,
     notes and the rocks themselves;
  f. any of the above made for downloadable content (DLC), expansions, updates, sequels or
     other games and products in the Rock and Found series.


2. IT IS VOLUNTARY

  - You contribute because you want to. Nobody assigns you work, sets you deadlines or asks
    for a set amount, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.
  - You are not an employee or contractor, and you are not paid for your contributions.
  - Andrei may choose to thank contributors later, for example with game keys or payment,
    but nothing in this agreement promises it. Any thank you is up to him.


3. WHAT YOU ALLOW

You keep the copyright in your contributions. You give Andrei, and anyone he works with to
make, publish or sell the game or its series (such as a store, a publisher or a porting
studio), permission to:

  - use, copy, publish and sell your contributions as part of the game, its demo, its
    updates, its DLC, its expansions and sequels, its store pages and its marketing, in
    every country, with no time limit and at no cost;
  - edit, shorten, correct or combine your contributions with other work;
  - replace them later, for example with a paid professional translation, or not use them
    at all.

You cannot take this permission back once your contribution has been included in a released
version of the game or of anything in section 1f. You will not object to the edits described
above, as far as the law where you live allows you to agree to that.


4. SUGGESTIONS AND IDEAS

If you suggest an idea, a change or an edit, Andrei may use it, change it or not use it, in
any of the ways in section 3, and owes you nothing for it beyond the credit in section 7. If
the game later contains something similar to an idea you suggested, that does not by itself
mean it came from you.


5. THE ROCK LAB IS OPEN

Rock and Found is built with the help of many free CC0 assets, and the Rock Lab gives back
the same way: Andrei plans to let anyone use rocks made in the Rock Lab in their own
projects, to help more games show rocks as they really are.

So the ROCKS made in the Rock Lab, meaning their 3D models, their baked textures and their
parameters (the numbers and settings that describe a rock's shape, color and look), may be
released to the public under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication
(creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). For your part in them:

  - You dedicate your contributions to those rocks to the public domain under CC0 1.0, from
    the moment Andrei releases them, and waive your rights in them as far as the law
    allows.
  - Once released, anyone may use, change and sell them for any purpose, including in
    commercial games, without asking and without credit. That cannot be taken back.
  - Nothing else is released this way: not your votes, orderings, notes or writing in the
    Rock Lab, not your other contributions, and not the game's or the Rock Lab's code,
    shaders, writing or other assets. Those are used only as section 3 describes.
  - Andrei may also publish code later to help other engines show the rocks. That code will
    carry its own license and is not part of this agreement.


6. WHAT YOU PROMISE

  - Your contributions are your own work, made by you.
  - They are NOT made with machine translation or AI tools, and are not copied from someone
    else's translation, text, model or data, unless that source is free to use for any
    purpose (such as CC0) and you tell Andrei where it came from.
  - You have the right to give the permissions above, and you are 18 or older.


7. CREDIT

  - You are credited in the game under the name you choose, or anonymously if you prefer.
    You can ask to change or remove your credit name in a future update.
  - If your work is later replaced or heavily edited, you stay credited for the version you
    contributed.
  - Under CC0, people who use Rock Lab rocks in their own projects are not required to
    credit you, though they may.


8. YOUR INFORMATION

Your name, contact address, the version you agreed to and when are kept only to credit you
and to show that you agreed. They are not shared or sold. To change your credit name or ask
a question, message Andrei through the Rock and Found Discord.


9. EVERYTHING ELSE

  - There is no warranty either way: your contributions are used as they are, and you are
    not responsible for how they are used.
  - This is the whole agreement about your contributions. If any part of it does not hold
    up where you live, the rest still applies.
